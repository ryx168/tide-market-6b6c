怎麼玩 How to play

用方向鍵、WASD 或螢幕上的方向盤在碼頭街上走動，也可以直接點地圖上的目的地。
Walk the dock street with the arrow keys, WASD or the on-screen D-pad, or tap a spot on the map.

走進四個攤位各聊一段，收下一枚紀念幣；集滿四枚再走上船，回合結束。
Walk into each of the four stalls to talk and take a token; with all four, board the ship to finish.

分數看時間：越快越高。對話時計時暫停。
Score is by time: faster is better. The clock pauses while you read.
